DigiNameCard – Privacy Policy
Last Updated: December 8, 2025
DigiNameCard (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and protect information when you use the DigiNameCard mobile application (“App”).
By downloading or using DigiNameCard, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect
DigiNameCard is designed with privacy as a core feature. We do not require user accounts, external servers, or third-party data storage.
Information You Input Manually
When you create or edit your digital business card or add contacts, the following information may be stored only on your device:
Name, job title, company


Phone number, email address, website links


Profile image or logo


Tags, notes, or additional contact details


Scanned business card information (OCR)


QR code information


Exported/imported vCard (.vcf), CSV, or other files


Automatically Collected Information
We may collect limited technical information through Apple's platform analytics (if enabled by the user in iOS settings), such as:
Device type


App performance metrics


Crashes and error logs


This information is anonymous and not linked to personal identity.

2. Data Storage & Security
Local Storage (Default)
All personal data and contacts are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your data.
iCloud Backup (Optional)
If enabled, your data may be backed up to your personal Apple iCloud account.
 We do not host or view backup data, and Apple’s iCloud security policies apply.
No External Servers
We do not collect, transmit, or store your data on any external server.


We do not sell, share, or mine personal information.


You retain full ownership and control of your data.

3. How We Use Information
We use your information only to operate the app features locally, such as:
Creating and displaying your digital business card


Managing contacts and scanned cards


Generating QR codes for sharing


Exporting or importing contacts


We do not use your contact data for analytics, advertising, or marketing.

4. Sharing of Information
DigiNameCard does not share information with third parties.
Data is only shared when you choose to export or send it (example: vCard/CSV sharing via email, text, AirDrop).
We never:
Sell personal data


Track users across apps or websites


Share data for advertising purposes
